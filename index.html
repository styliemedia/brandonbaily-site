About me

On the mic and on the bike

I'm an arena MC and motorbike stunt rider based in Kent. Most weekends from spring to autumn you'll find me at a county show, festival or motor event somewhere in the UK, either holding the microphone or holding a wheelie.

Because I ride as well as commentate, I know what's coming next in a stunt show, so I can build the crowd at the right moment and talk people through every trick.

  • Arena MC

    Live commentary that keeps the crowd with you all day, from the first act to the last.

  • Stunt performer

    ATV stunt rider and car stunt driver, performing with Stunt Shows UK and Moto-Stunts International. The UK's best two-wheel car stunt driver on grass.

  • Photography

    Action and event photography from inside the arena, by someone who knows exactly where the stunt will land. More at @styliemedia.
